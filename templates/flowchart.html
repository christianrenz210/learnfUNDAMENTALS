Flowchart Builder

Draw a flowchart by adding shapes, connecting them with arrows, and editing labels. Your work is saved automatically on this device.

Add:
Tap a shape above to add it, then drag to move. Double-click a shape to rename it.
Try it yourself

Pick a challenge and build the flowchart, then compare with the code in the lesson:

  • "Is a number even or odd?"
  • "Find the largest of three numbers."
  • "Check if a year is a leap year."
  • "Login: verify username and password."
How to use
  1. Click a shape button to drop it on the canvas.
  2. Drag shapes to position them.
  3. Click Connect, then click two shapes to draw an arrow.
  4. Double-click a shape to edit its text.
  5. Select a shape and press Delete to remove it.
