Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about CodeFundamentals.

Each lesson ends with a short quiz. You get one attempt per quiz, and your score is saved on your dashboard and added to the leaderboard. You must take the quiz before you can mark the lesson as complete — the "Mark as Complete" button stays locked until your score is saved. Finishing every lesson in a section unlocks that section's 15-question long quiz (2 attempts, 20 seconds per question) — your best score is kept, and you can review your answers afterwards.

Users are ranked by their total points: quiz scores plus lab bonuses. Each SQL Injection Lab you complete is worth 2 points, so labs can boost your ranking quickly. There is also a separate leaderboard for each lesson so you can see who topped that lesson.

E.R.E.N (Educational Response Engine for Novices) is an AI assistant that appears on every page while you are logged in — just click the robot button at the bottom-right corner. Type a question like "What are variables?" or "Explain loops", tap a suggested chip, and it will answer with examples in Python, C++, Java, and C. It can also tell you your lesson progress, explain the quizzes and the long quiz, show how the leaderboard is ranked, and turn any topic into a presentation you can download as PowerPoint.

There are 35 lessons available in four sections: 11 programming fundamentals lessons (Python, C++, Java), 6 advanced fundamentals lessons (GitHub, Deployment, Render, Netlify, WordPress, TypeScript), 8 advanced database lessons (SQL), and 10 SQL injection lessons — each with its own interactive practice lab.

Yes. Complete all lessons in a section to unlock that section's certificate, and complete every lesson to unlock the Full Course certificate — both can be printed or saved as PDF. You also earn a badge for every completed lesson, plus gold badges for finishing each section and the full course.

Every SQL Injection lesson has its own Practice Lab — a safe playground that simulates a vulnerable database entirely in your browser, so no real SQL is ever executed. In each lab you try login-bypass payloads, steal data with UNION attacks, compare the vulnerable code with parameterized queries, and finish with a Final Challenge: type your own payload (no copy-paste) using up to 3 hints. Once you solve a lab, it shows a green "Lab Completed" checkmark on your dashboard and adds 2 leaderboard points.

Coins are the reward you earn on the site. You get 10 coins every time you mark a lesson as complete, and your balance is always visible in the navbar and on your dashboard. Coins are saved to your account, so they stay with you across visits.

Lessons unlock automatically when you complete the previous lesson. You can also spend coins to skip ahead: from the dashboard, click the "Unlock with coins" button on a locked lesson. The price depends on the lesson's difficulty — Easy lessons cost 10 coins, Medium cost 20, and Hard cost 30. You can see each lesson's difficulty on its card.

Ask E.R.E.N for a presentation, for example "Make me a PowerPoint about loops" or "Create slides about SQL injection". It will show the slides right in the chat — each with key points, a runnable code sample, and its sample output — and a "Download PPT" button appears at the top of the reply. Click it and a .pptx file is saved to your device, named after the topic. The file opens in PowerPoint, Google Slides, or any office app, and you can edit it freely.
