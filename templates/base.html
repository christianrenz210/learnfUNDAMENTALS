E.R.E.N
Educational Response Engine for Novices
History
The Story of E.R.E.N

It was 2 AM in a cramped dorm room. A first-year computer science student had just failed the same quiz about loops for the third time. Frustrated, exhausted, and running out of coffee, the student had an idea: what if the lesson could explain itself the way a friend would?

So they stayed up night after night, writing a program that answered questions patiently, with examples and zero judgment. It began small — just a few rules and a few lessons. But every time a classmate asked something it could not answer, a new rule was added.

Hundreds of questions later, the little engine had learned how to explain variables, loops, functions, and every other concept in this course — the way a patient friend would.

E.R.E.N stands for Educational Response Engine for Novices. But really, it just wants you to learn one thing at a time — so you never have to be stuck at 2 AM.
